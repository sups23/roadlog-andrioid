# Pedestrian Gap Acceptance at Unsignalized Intersections — Dukem City, Ethiopia

## Citation
Search title: "Pedestrian Gap Acceptance Behavior at Un-Signalized Intersections under Mixed Traffic Conditions: A Case Study at Dukem City, Ethiopia."

## Core purpose
The study models pedestrian gap acceptance at unsignalized intersections in Dukem City, Ethiopia, using regression analysis.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Regression modelling:** Shows how to relate gap acceptance to explanatory variables such as traffic flow, pedestrian age, gender, and group size.
- **Mixed traffic:** Relevant to contexts with high two-wheeler and informal vehicle presence.
- **Developing-city case:** Another example from outside the developed-world literature.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Demonstrates that a single-city case study can contribute useful behavioural insights.
- Regression is possible even with observational data if you code enough variables.
- Gender and age effects are commonly significant.

## Limitations
- Specific to Dukem; context differs from Kathmandu.
