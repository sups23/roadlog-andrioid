# Simulation of Signalized Intersection with Non-Lane-Based Heterogeneous Traffic

## Citation
Search title: "Simulation of Signalized Intersection with Non-Lane-Based Heterogeneous Traffic." *Transportation Research Record*. DOI: 10.1177/03611981231211317.

## Core purpose
The paper develops a simulation model for a signalized intersection that captures non-lane-based movement and heterogeneous vehicle types, including seepage behaviour.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Non-lane-based traffic:** Describes how vehicles move without strict lane discipline — common in Kathmandu.
- **Seepage behaviour:** Two-wheelers squeezing between larger vehicles.
- **Heterogeneity:** Different vehicle sizes, speeds, and manoeuvrability.
- **Influence zone of intersections:** Area where vehicles adjust speed and position before/after the intersection.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Provides vocabulary and concepts for describing what you actually see.
- Helps explain why Western traffic models fail in Kathmandu.
- You can observe and count seepage, lane-splitting, and queue-jumping at your intersections.

## Limitations
- Simulation-focused, not observational.
- Requires calibration data you may not have.

## Suggested use
Use to frame the context: your observations capture the real-world behaviour that such simulations try to model.
