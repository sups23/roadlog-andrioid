# Chandra & Shrivastava — Indian Mixed Traffic Studies

## Citation
Chandra, S., & Shrivastava, A. K. Various papers on mixed traffic flow, speed, and intersection behaviour in India. Search these authors in Google Scholar.

## Core purpose
This body of work models speed, flow, gap acceptance, and intersection performance under Indian mixed traffic conditions.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Speed-flow relationships:** How different vehicle types interact in dense traffic.
- **Gap acceptance:** Driver and pedestrian gap acceptance in heterogeneous conditions.
- **Capacity analysis:** How intersections perform when lane discipline is weak.
- **Empirical calibration:** Data-driven parameters for Indian conditions.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Indian mixed-traffic research is the closest methodological cousin to Kathmandu.
- Their methods for observing vehicle interactions can be adapted for pedestrians.
- Provides context for why standard Western models need modification.

## Limitations
- Driver-oriented rather than pedestrian-oriented.
- Multiple papers; no single summary reference.

## Suggested use
Search for specific papers by these authors on gap acceptance or intersection behaviour. Cite them to justify a South Asian methodological approach.
