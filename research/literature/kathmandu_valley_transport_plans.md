# Kathmandu Valley Development Authority / Kathmandu Metropolitan City Transport Plans

## Citation
Kathmandu Valley Development Authority (KVDA), Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC), and related planning documents. Titles may include *Transport Master Plan*, *Strategic Urban Transport Plan*, or *Road Safety Plan*.

## Core purpose
These plans outline official goals, projects, and policies for transport and road safety in the Kathmandu Valley.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Policy context:** What the city says it wants to improve.
- **Project pipeline:** Upcoming interventions that could serve as natural experiments.
- **Identified problem areas:** Corridors or intersections flagged for improvement.
- **Design standards:** What infrastructure is planned or required.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Helps connect your micro-behavioural study to city-level policy.
- Useful for discussing recommendations.
- May reveal intersections where changes are planned, enabling before-after research.

## Limitations
- Plans may be outdated or not fully implemented.
- May not be freely available online.

## Suggested use
Search KVDA and KMC websites or reports. Cite relevant plans to show policy relevance.
