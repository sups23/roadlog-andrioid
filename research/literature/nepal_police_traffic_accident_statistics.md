# Nepal Police Annual Traffic Accident Statistics

## Citation
Nepal Police. Annual reports on traffic accidents and road safety. Available through Nepal Police publications or government portals.

## Core purpose
These reports provide annual counts of traffic accidents, injuries, and fatalities in Nepal, often disaggregated by vehicle type and road user.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Crash context:** Shows the scale of road danger in Nepal.
- **Vehicle involvement:** Which vehicle types are most often involved in pedestrian crashes.
- **Temporal patterns:** Monthly or seasonal variations in crashes.
- **Validation:** Helps argue that your observed conflicts reflect real risk.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Useful background for the problem statement.
- Can help you select intersections near reported crash hotspots.
- Provides comparison data for interpreting your conflict counts.

## Limitations
- Under-reporting is common, especially for non-fatal crashes.
- May not distinguish intersection vs. mid-block crashes.

## Suggested use
Use as supporting evidence in your introduction. Do not rely on it as your primary data source.
