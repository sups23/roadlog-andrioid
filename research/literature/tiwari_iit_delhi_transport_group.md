# Tiwari & IIT Delhi Transport Group — Pedestrian and NMT Safety in Indian Cities

## Citation
Tiwari, G., and colleagues at IIT Delhi. Various papers on pedestrian safety, non-motorized transport, and intersection design in Indian cities.

## Core purpose
This research group has produced a large body of work on road safety, pedestrian behaviour, and sustainable transport in Indian cities.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Pedestrian exposure and risk:** Links time spent on the road to crash risk.
- **NMT-friendly design:** How street design affects cyclists and pedestrians.
- **Indian empirical data:** Real-world behaviour studies from cities with traffic conditions similar to Kathmandu.
- **Policy relevance:** Strong connections to planning and engineering practice.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Provides a regional authority to cite for safety and behaviour topics.
- Demonstrates that pedestrian safety is a serious research agenda in South Asia.
- Many papers use observational and video-based methods.

## Limitations
- India-specific; Kathmandu has unique topography, governance, and vehicle mix.
- Multiple papers; you need to find the specific one relevant to your topic.

## Suggested use
Search Google Scholar for "Tiwari pedestrian safety India" or "Tiwari intersection design India." Select 1–2 papers closest to your topic.
