# McShane & Roess — Traffic Engineering

## Citation
McShane, W. R., & Roess, R. P. (1990). *Traffic Engineering*. Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice Hall. (Later editions exist.)

## Core purpose
A standard textbook covering traffic engineering fundamentals: flow theory, intersection design, signals, capacity, and safety.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Intersection types:** Signalized, unsignalized, roundabouts, and their characteristics.
- **Capacity and delay:** Basic concepts for measuring intersection performance.
- **Traffic control devices:** Signals, signs, markings.
- **Safety fundamentals:** Conflict points, sight distance, and design principles.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Provides technical vocabulary and standard definitions.
- Helps you describe your intersections in engineering terms.
- Useful if your committee includes engineers.

## Limitations
- US-oriented; many standard solutions do not apply to Kathmandu.
- Older editions may lack modern topics like shared space or bike lanes.

## Suggested use
Use as a reference for basic terminology. Do not treat it as the final word for your local context.
