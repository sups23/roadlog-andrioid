# Sadik-Khan & Solomonow — Streetfight

## Citation
Sadik-Khan, J., & Solomonow, S. (2016). *Streetfight: Handbook for an Urban Revolution*. New York: Viking.

## Core purpose
A practical account of how New York City redesigned streets to prioritize pedestrians, cyclists, and transit.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Before-after examples:** How intersection redesign changed behaviour.
- **Quick, cheap interventions:** Paint, bollards, signal timing.
- **Political and design lessons:** How to build support for street changes.
- **Data-driven approach:** Using counts and observations to justify design.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Shows that small physical changes can produce measurable behavioural changes.
- Inspires before-after observational designs.
- Useful if your recommendations include low-cost intersection improvements.

## Limitations
- New York context with strong institutions and budgets.
- Less about behavioural theory, more about implementation.

## Suggested use
Use in the conclusion or recommendations section to suggest practical improvements based on your observations.
