# Gorrini, Vizzari & Bandini — Towards Modelling Pedestrian-Vehicle Interactions

## Citation
Gorrini, A., Vizzari, G., & Bandini, S. (2016). "Towards Modelling Pedestrian-Vehicle Interactions: Empirical Study on Urban Unsignalized Intersection." *Proceedings of the 8th International Conference on Pedestrian and Evacuation Dynamics (PED2016)*, Hefei, China. Also available on arXiv:1610.07892.

## Core purpose
The paper reports a video-based observation campaign at an unsignalized zebra crossing in Milan, focusing on interactions between pedestrians and vehicles, with special attention to elderly pedestrians.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Pedestrian-vehicle interaction taxonomy:** Categories of interaction types and their frequencies.
- **Elderly pedestrian behaviour:** Older pedestrians showed more cautious behaviour and longer waiting times.
- **Level of service (LOS):** Relates observed interactions to pedestrian comfort and safety.
- **Volume measurement:** How vehicular and pedestrian traffic volumes were collected and analyzed.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Demonstrates that a small video-based observation at one intersection can produce publishable findings.
- Elderly-focused analysis is replicable and socially relevant.
- You can compare vulnerable groups (elderly, children, women) at your intersections.

## Limitations
- Single location in a European city.
- Mild traffic conditions compared to Kathmandu.

## Suggested use
Use this study as a template for your own observation campaign: video recording, interaction coding, volume counts, and demographic classification.
