# Whyte — The Social Life of Small Urban Spaces

## Citation
Whyte, W. H. (1980). *The Social Life of Small Urban Spaces*. New York: Project for Public Spaces.

## Core purpose
A classic observational study of how people use small urban plazas and spaces in New York City, based on time-lapse film analysis.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **Observation methodology:** Time-lapse photography and behavioural coding.
- **Where people stand:** How sun, shade, edges, and obstacles shape behaviour.
- **Social behaviour:** People-watching, group formation, and territoriality.
- **Design-behaviour link:** How physical features predict human actions.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- You can use time-lapse or video to observe pedestrian positioning at intersections.
- Useful for topics like “where people stand while waiting to cross.”
- Demonstrates that simple observation can yield powerful insights.

## Limitations
- Focuses on plazas, not streets.
- Older work; urban contexts have changed.

## Suggested use
Use as a methodological inspiration. Adapt Whyte’s observational spirit to intersection waiting and crossing behaviour.
