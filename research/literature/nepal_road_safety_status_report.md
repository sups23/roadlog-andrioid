# Nepal Road Safety Status Report

## Citation
Department of Transport Management (DoTM), Nepal / World Bank. Various editions of the *Road Safety Status Report* or *Road Safety Action Plan* for Nepal.

## Core purpose
These government and donor reports summarize road crash trends, vulnerable road users, black spots, and policy priorities in Nepal.

## What is useful for intersection observation research
- **National crash statistics:** Context for why pedestrian safety matters in Nepal.
- **Black spot lists:** May help you identify dangerous intersections to observe.
- **Vulnerable road users:** Data on pedestrians, motorcyclists, and cyclists as the main victims.
- **Policy gaps:** Shows that official data are incomplete, justifying observational research.

## Key takeaways for a Kathmandu project
- Provides national justification for your local study.
- Helps you argue that Kathmandu intersections are under-researched.
- Useful for the “problem statement” section of your thesis.

## Limitations
- Data quality and completeness vary.
- May not provide detailed intersection-level information.

## Suggested use
Cite in your introduction to establish the importance of pedestrian safety in Nepal and the need for local behavioural evidence.
