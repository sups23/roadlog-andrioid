**“Passenger-Annotated Micro-Delay and Road Roughness Atlas of Kathmandu Urban Corridors”**
## Short Research Question
How do road roughness and other observable street-level factors contribute to micro-delays experienced by passengers on Kathmandu’s public transport and urban road corridors?
## Problem Statement
Urban delay in Kathmandu is often measured through overall travel time, congestion perception, or broad traffic conditions. However, these measures do not explain the specific street-level causes of delay experienced during actual trips. Delays may result from road roughness, potholes, speed breakers, bus stopping, pedestrian crossings, roadside parking, vendors, intersections, and turning vehicles.
Road roughness is especially important because it can directly force vehicles to slow down, brake, change lanes, or create local bottlenecks. Yet it is rarely studied as a measurable cause of travel delay in Kathmandu. By collecting GPS, speed, smartphone motion-sensor data, and passenger annotations from both public buses and other urban trips, this study will identify where micro-delays occur, what causes them, and how strongly poor road surface conditions contribute to delay.
## Objectives
1. To identify and classify micro-delay events on selected Kathmandu corridors using passenger GPS traces, speed profiles, and real-time field annotations.
2. To measure the relationship between smartphone-detected road roughness and passenger-experienced delay across public bus and other urban trips.
## Expected Outcomes
1. A GIS-based map of micro-delay hotspots and their observed causes.
2. A road roughness hotspot map generated from smartphone sensor data.
3. Evidence on whether and where rough road conditions significantly contribute to speed reduction and delay.
4. A ranked comparison of major delay causes such as road roughness, bus stopping, parking, pedestrian activity, intersections, and encroachment.
5. Practical corridor-level recommendations for road maintenance, bus stop management, and traffic improvement.
